Technical Specifications & Horology

Every Nomad strap originates in the historic Horween Tannery on Elston Avenue in Chicago, founded in 1905. Cordovan requires at least six months of vegetable tanning in proprietary bark liquors followed by hot-stuffing with artisanal greases and waxes.

Hand-Stitched Saddle Method

Sewn with twin needles on a wooden stitching clam using wax-saturated French linen cord. Each loop knots independently, ensuring that even under severe friction, the strap seam can never unravel.

Zermatt Leather Underlay

Lined exclusively with calfskin from Tanneries Haas in France, specifically developed to resist wrist perspiration and sebum without discoloring or stiffening with time.

Our strap ends are curved and micro-beveled to eliminate case rub on reference cases with tight lug tolerances:

  • Rolex: Submariner (20mm), Datejust 36 (20mm), Daytona (20mm), Explorer 39 (20mm), GMT-Master II (20mm).
  • Omega: Speedmaster Professional Moonwatch (20mm), Seamaster Diver 300M (20mm), Aqua Terra 150M (20mm).
  • Tudor & Cartier: Black Bay Fifty-Eight (20mm), Black Bay 41 (22mm), Santos Medium (Custom adapter), Tank Louis.
  • Seiko & Grand Seiko: Heritage 44GS (19mm), SBGA211 Snowflake (20mm), SKX007 Diver (22mm).

Each strap is vacuum-sealed in oxygen-depleted packaging with a cedar block to preserve the tannery aroma. Dispatched via DHL Express or FedEx Priority within 24 hours of order allocation.
