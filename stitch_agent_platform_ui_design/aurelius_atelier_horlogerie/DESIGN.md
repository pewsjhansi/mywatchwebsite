## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the discipline and uncompromising craftsmanship of haute horlogerie. Built for an ultra-luxury strap atelier, the visual narrative evokes the tactile intimacy of bespoke leatherwork, satin-brushed metallics, and Swiss precision engineering. The target audience comprises discerning horological collectors, connoisseurs of rare materials (alligator, cordovan, vulcanized rubber, Milanese mesh), and design purists seeking tailor-made pairings for exceptional timepieces.

The design movement combines **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Architectonic Precision**:
- Extreme balance and structural restraint: heavy, breathing negative space that treats product displays like museum vitrines.
- High-contrast typography alternating between literary serifs and technical, spaced grotesques.
- Tactile delicacy: gossamer hairlines (0.5px to 1px), muted precious-metal accents, and velvet-smooth transitions replace loud commercial cues.
- The UI recedes into the background, ensuring high-resolution macro photography of hand-stitching, bevels, and grain remains the hero.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from watchmaker benches, raw noble metals, and untreated vegetable-tanned hides:

- **Primary (`#0D0D0E` - Obsidian Black):** The definitive ground for typography, structured frames, and ultra-high-contrast actions.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880` - Champagne Gold):** A desaturated, metallic brushed-champagne tone reserved for micro-accents, monograms, active state pips, and prestige badges. Avoid hyper-saturated gold gradients; favor flat or subtle directional metallic sheen. An auxiliary highlight of `#D4AF37` is permitted exclusively for active focus rings or metal hardware previews.
- **Tertiary (`#2A2A2E` - Muted Graphite Slate):** Used for secondary typography, technical specifications (lug widths, taper metrics), and dark-mode auxiliary containers.
- **Neutral (`#F5F3EF` - Warm Alabaster/Sand):** Softens the visual impact of clinical white, framing strap photography in warmth reminiscent of archive paper and calfskin linings.
- **Canvas Base (`#FBFBFA` - Soft Pure Off-White):** The primary view surface, delivering crisp optical purity without clinical glare.
- **Structural Dividers (`#E5E2DC` - Linen Border Tint):** 1px hairlines that segment categories, product matrices, and bespoke configurator panels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges high-end editorial gravitas and modern industrial taxonomy:

- **Display & Headings:** Set in *Playfair Display* with transitional italic accents for artisanal notes. Headlines preserve tight line-heights and slight negative tracking at large scales to evoke vintage European watch catalogs.
- **Body Text:** Set in *Inter* at light (`300`) and regular (`400`) weights. Generous line-heights ensure effortless legibility when reading long-form provenance stories.
- **Labels, Badges, and Metadata:** Set in uppercase *Inter* with expanded tracking (`0.18em`). Used exclusively for collection categories, lug-width badges (`20MM / 16MM`), leather origins, and currency displays.
- **Technical Readouts:** Standardized in tabular-friendly, spaced sans-serif for dimensions, buckle finishes, and reference numbers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an architectural gallery, utilizing a strict 12-column responsive grid anchored by generous outer negative space:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, max-width 1440px centered, `margin-desktop: 5rem`, `gutter-desktop: 2.5rem`. Asymmetrical splits (e.g., 7 columns for strap macro shots, 5 columns for bespoke configurator parameters) emphasize custom craftsmanship.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin-tablet: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Two-up product cards with sticky side drawer filters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Full-bleed carousel cards for material macro details with persistent bottom action rails for sizing selection.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Section spacing scales dynamically from `space-xl` (mobile) to `8rem` (desktop) between editorial chapters, maintaining quiet pauses between curated strap collections.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows and synthetic blur effects, embracing **Tonal Layering** and **Precision Razor Borders**:

- **Surfaces:** Depth is achieved by shifting backgrounds between Pure Off-White (`#FBFBFA`) and Warm Alabaster (`#F5F3EF`), enclosed in 1px borders colored `#E5E2DC`.
- **Modals & Drawers:** Layered using subtle obsidian ambient cast: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(13, 13, 14, 0.08)`. Never harsh, purely volumetric.
- **Hover Micro-Elevation:** Product cards shift border color from `#E5E2DC` to Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`) while raising image scale subtly (1.015x) over 400ms cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1.0) without vertical translation.
- **Floating Controls (Configurator / Sizing Dock):** Backdropped with a frosted satin film: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(251, 251, 250, 0.85); border: 1px solid rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The foundational geometry is **Razor Sharp (`0px`)**, honoring traditional Swiss drafting sheets, sapphire crystal bevels, and guilloché patterns:

- Primary structural containers, product vitrines, filters, input fields, and bespoke configurator steps feature crisp 90-degree right angles (`0px`).
- **Selective Curvature Exception (Pills):** Lug-width chips, material swatch indicators, and quick-add actions utilize full circular caps (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create an intentional counterpoint that mimics watch cases, bezel rings, and strap pin holes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury (Sharp):** Background `#0D0D0E`, text `#FBFBFA`, `0px` radius, uppercase `label-caps` font, padding `18px 36px`. Hover: Background `#C5A880`, text `#0D0D0E`, transition 300ms ease.
- **Secondary Luxury (Outlined Sharp):** 1px solid `#0D0D0E`, text `#0D0D0E`, background transparent. Hover: Background `#F5F3EF`, border-color `#C5A880`.
- **Pill Variant (Atelier Accent):** Full pill (`9999px`), 1px solid `#E5E2DC`, text `#0D0D0E`, padding `10px 22px`. Reserved for quick customization, "Configure Bespoke", or tactile category filters.

### Swatches & Material Badges
- **Color/Leather Swatches:** Dual-ring presentation: 24px diameter circles displaying genuine grain preview. Active state displays an outer `#C5A880` 1px offset ring with 2px white gap.
- **Lug Width Chips:** Segmented pill pills (`18mm`, `19mm`, `20mm`, `22mm`) in `#F5F3EF` with `technical-mono` font; active state shifts to `#0D0D0E` with `#FBFBFA` text.

### Product Vitrine Cards
- Minimum 4:5 vertical aspect ratio. Edge-to-edge off-white background with `#E5E2DC` baseline separation.
- Title in `headline-sm`, subtitle/leather origin in `label-caps` muted `#2A2A2E`, price in `technical-mono`.
- Hardware preview toggle (Brushed Steel, Yellow Gold, Rose Gold, DLC Black) pinned unobtrusively in top-right corner.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat bottom hairlines (1px `#E5E2DC`) or fully boxed sharp containers with zero borders except a perimeter hairline.
- Focus state: hairlines transform to `#C5A880` without aggressive glow rings. Placeholder text in `#2A2A2E` at 40% opacity.

### Bespoke Strap Measurement Configurator
- Step-by-step horizontal strip with Roman-numeral markers (`I. LEATHER`, `II. LUG & BUCKLE`, `III. STITCHING`).
- Real-time technical line illustration showing strap taper down to 0.5mm precision.
